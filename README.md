### HCMC Street Greening Dashboard

**From street-view research to an interactive greening assessment tool.**

### PosturePal

**A posture-awareness and focus companion prototype.**

A product exploration for people studying or working at a desk, combining camera-based posture recognition with reminders and focus feedback.

- **My contribution:** Product positioning, user journeys, feature architecture, posture rules, progression design, and the React + MediaPipe prototype framework.
- **Product decisions:** Persistence thresholds and cooldowns to reduce repeated reminders; fallback states for low-confidence detection, occlusion, and absence.
- **Methods & tools:** React, MediaPipe Pose, interaction design, state-based feedback.
- **Scope:** V1 prototype; recognition accuracy and long-term habit outcomes have not been established.

围绕“校准—识别—持续性判断—分级提醒—专注结算—成长反馈”设计产品闭环，关注提醒时机、异常兜底和长期使用体验。

[Live prototype / 在线原型](https://posture-pal-2jc4.vercel.app)
